You can now open two documents side by side with the new split view, making it easy to reference one document while working in another. Open a second pane – choose "Open in split view" from a document or collection's menu, or from the command bar Quick shortcuts – modifier-click any link in the sidebar or editor (in the desktop app), or press Cmd+Enter / Ctrl+Enter from the command menu, to open it in the split pane instead of navigating away Resizable panes – drag the divider between panes to adjust their widths, or double-click it to reset to an even split Each pane keeps its own navigation
