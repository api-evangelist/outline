It's been a long time coming – commenting is finally available in Outline, opening up a whole new world of use cases and collaboration! While we iron out the kinks it's disabled by default, but you can enable the feature for your team in Settings -> Features -> Commenting . Once enabled you'll see a new comments sidebar become available.
